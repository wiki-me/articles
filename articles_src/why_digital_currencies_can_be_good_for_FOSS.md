# why digital currencies can be good for FOSS

One of the most well known Platform for donating to FOSS (Liberapay) [cites](https://en.liberapay.com/about/faq#fees) an average fees of 3.1% for its stripe payments and 5.1% for its paypal payments.

If you are given a income worth a year of work. that means about 11 days in a year goes to the paymen processor.

Ripple has a market capitalisation [making](https://companiesmarketcap.com/assets-by-market-cap/) it one of the largest assets in the world , bigger then companies such as verizon , xiaomi and charles schwab. and has a reported transaction cost of between [$0.0011](https://cryptomus.com/blog/top-10-crypto-with-lowest-transaction-fees) and [$0.0002 per transaction](https://fuze.finance/blog/cryptocurrencies-with-lowest-transaction-fees/)

Nano is also reportedly eco friendly and has reportedly transaction fees that are [free](https://cryptomus.com/blog/top-10-crypto-with-lowest-transaction-fees) or [near zero](https://fuze.finance/blog/cryptocurrencies-with-lowest-transaction-fees/).

Besides saving money and not given it to maybe questionable corporations. this could cause a [halo effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halo_effect) and when people will see that a open source solution like a digital currency is significantly better then a closed source solution. this might make them think FOSS might be better then closed source and use and eventually support other FOSS projects.

There is already some adoption of digital currencies by large open source projects. for example [signal](https://signal.org/donate/) and [the internet archive](https://archive.org/donate/cryptocurrency).
