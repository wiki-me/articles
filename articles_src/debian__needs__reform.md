Right now. according to the debian constitution. only the debian project leader (DPL) gets to decide how money is spent.

The issue of making the debian project pay for developers has been debated for years. Making that decision can be anxiety provoking. the DPL might worry people will be angry if it turns out that money will not be spent well and the project will not be successful. This could prevent the DPL from taking calculated risks (which is a problem due to [risk aversion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk_aversion)).

That is why most companies and non profits as far as i can tell have a board of directors. if multiple people agree to spend the money that could help reduce the anxiety.

Making a decision on what to fund actually involves a making a prediction on what would help the project most. the [good judgement project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Good_Judgment_Project) researched how to predict events more successfully and one of its conclusions is that  [using teams is better](https://www.leadershipreview.net/superforecasting-how-to-make-winning-judgment-calls/#:~:text=In%20each%20year%20of%20the,balances%2C%20thus%20achieving%20enhanced%20accuracy.):

> In each year of the IARAP’s tournament, GJP forecasters working collaboratively in teams consistently outperformed individual forecasters. By discussing among themselves, forecasters could eliminate any unconscious biases and maintain effective checks and balances, thus achieving enhanced accuracy.
