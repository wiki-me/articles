I think there is a pattern when starting to contribute to FOSS. i remember seeing at least one instance of it. Basically something like fear of failure. people are afraid the project won't gain traction, ridicule from other developers etc.

I posted a response about it and got really good feedback. I am posting it here in case other people will find it helpful. i am not a mental health professional, so this is not a "reliable source" and i recommend doing your own research.

the response:

And you should care what people you don't know on the internet think because... ?

social media is not a reliable source. it can expose you to new information, but it is not reliable .

Feedback is how you learn. how you find out what works and just as importantly what don't work.

i think there was a study that showed that people that take a course on decision making have lower anxiety. it makes sense, once you do a risk-reward analysis and consider alternatives it makes you accept the risk and act despite it. realizing the anxiety is unhelpful or ineffective repeatedly leads to [Desensitization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desensitization_(psychology)) and eventually reduces it.

with that said, there might be some underlining issue and you need to work on self regulation skills. using stuff like CBT/ACT/NSDR/meditation/stoicism. there is a open source test for the big five personality [test](https://bigfive-test.com/). if you score high on neuroticism, it might indicate you should work on it.
