# Wikipedia governance model is highly vulnerable to foreign propaganda 

To vote for the wikipedia board you need to make a [certain amount of edits](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Wikimedia_Foundation_elections/2025/Voter_eligibility_guidelines). someone like russia , china or iran could easy hire people and make them have multiple accounts which will give them multiple votes. using stuff like AI or chatgpt makes this even cheaper. This makes wikipedia a attractive target for those wanting to spread anti western propaganda.

I propose a improvement. the people voting should only be people who donated like on codeberg. maybe even having something like real election where only people in free countries can vote after their ID is checked. this could be experimented with by creating a fork of wikipedia and seeing if the fork becomes less anti western and more reliable using a scientific research methodology or benchmarks.
